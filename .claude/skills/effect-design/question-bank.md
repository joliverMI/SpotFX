# Question bank — by kind, by design-tree branch

How to use (SKILL.md step 3): pick the KIND block, walk the BRANCHES in
order, drop any question the facts step already settled, and ask the
frontier in numbered rounds, one recommended answer each, worded so "yes"
accepts it. The recommended answers below are his recorded defaults — the
rulings he has made more than once. Each question carries the sessions it
came from (short names of past plan directories) so a future reader can
see why it is here. `learnings.md` says which ones changed a plan and
which wasted a round; promote and demote here accordingly.

Conventions: 🅰 = he almost always takes this answer (ask only to confirm,
or fold into the plan's defaults and move on). ❗ = this question has
changed a plan; ask it early. 👁 = visual — ask beside renders, never in
words. ✂ = usually unnecessary; ask only when the facts leave it open.

---

## 0. Every kind — the opening round

These settle the tree's root. Ask them first, with the intake
classification said back in one line.

- ❗ **Copy or modify?** For a new effect "like X": copy X into a new
  module and scene, or change X itself? 🅰 Copy (Sauron: "Copy"), so his
  tuned X stays his. (sauron-eye-plan)
- ❗ **Which target first?** Crystal only now and a strips concept shown
  for later, or crystal + strips + singles together? 🅰 Crystal now, show
  a basic strips concept, singles follow the shared layer. (sauron-eye-plan,
  strip-phase-plan)
- ❗ **What is the ONE visible claim?** State back the sentence he will
  check in the room ("the star grows from the centre", "three fish become
  three orbits") and ask him to confirm it is the claim. Everything else
  is a means. (star-plan-rev, shoal-burst-plan)
- 🅰 **Build then tune, before any scene moves.** Confirm the tuning gate:
  the effect is built and registered, he tunes defaults live, THEN the
  scene is seeded / migrated. Never ask whether to skip it. (ADDING_EFFECTS,
  single-led-power-plan, sauron-eye-plan, fish-swim-plan)
- ✂ **Name?** Only when the name is not already in his ask. (sauron: "yes")

## 1. Look and motion

### Matrix effects (crystal-mapper)

- 👁❗ **Which of the rendered looks?** Show today + each option on the
  real 976 cells, same seed, same music, on one of HIS colour sets with
  backgrounds off. Ask "A, B or C" under the tiles. He picks from the
  pictures and often from the option built on his own hint (STAR lull B,
  drop D3). (star-plan, sauron-eye-plan, predrop-plan, transitions-drop-plan)
- 👁❗ **Does anything "just appear"?** Any frame where lit cells jump is a
  defect to him. Recommended: pieces/front grow in over hundreds of ms,
  reveal gated radially from the core, never a global fade-in. Show the
  largest one-frame lit-cell climb beside the tile. (star-plan-rev)
- ❗ **Continuity of motion across a change.** Recommended: velocity and
  heading continuous through any hand-off; moving targets are met where
  they will be, not where they were. (transitions-plan-rev, shoal-burst-plan)
- **Where is the effect's centre / "eye"?** Any shock, bloom or ring
  originates from the effect's own form (horizon ring, tether ring, iris
  rim), never from the panel centre or the panel perimeter. 🅰 Yes.
  (transitions-plan-rev3, star-plan)
- **How many, how big, how fast at rest / at full music?** Give today's
  numbers and a proposed range; recommend a param with today's value as
  the escape hatch. 🅰 "First guesses for his eye; tune live."
  (fish-swim-plan, bh-infall-plan, shoal-burst-plan)
- **Realism cue wanted?** When his ask uses a real-world image (pond fish,
  searching, flames licking), ask which cue carries it (the tail stroke,
  the pause-and-turn, the reach to the hex edge) — one cue, visible at
  several music levels. (fish-swim-plan, fish-lull-plan, sauron-eye-plan)
- ✂ **Beat modulates which dimension?** (width only / width + height) 🅰
  "As prototyped." (sauron-eye-plan)
- ✂ **Shape morph on a param re-roll, and how long?** 🅰 Yes, ~0.6 s.
  (star-plan)

### Strip effects

- ❗ **Charge look: more colour and activity, never white.** Recommended:
  hue-kept gain, set-colour fill, higher counts/rates; retire any white
  halo or additive flood. 🅰 Yes (he wrote the rule). (strip-plan-rev3,
  strip-phase-plan)
- **Which strips go to black in the lull, and what stays?** 🅰 All black at
  the shared dark point, except an ember-level trace where the effect has
  one (Fireworks). (strip-phase-plan)
- **Does the strip's phase ride into the crystal through the mapping?**
  For STAR-like mappings: charge and drop ride in, the strip's LULL does
  not (the crystal mapping handles the lull). ❗ He overrode the plain
  "yes" here. (star-plan)
- **House modes untouched?** 🅰 Strip phases hold back under a house mode.
  (strip-phase-plan)

### Singles (Hue bulbs, single-pixel WLEDs)

- **Colour source:** 🅰 his hand-picked colour per set; fill in only where
  missing and in rainbow sets; never derive from the strips automatically.
  (single-led-power-plan)
- **Rainbow on Hue:** 🅰 all bulbs one colour, jumping mostly on beats.
  (single-led-power-plan)
- **Calm music:** 🅰 subtle but visible; a minimum pulse and a brighter
  resting level because dim bulbs have few steps. (single-led-power-plan)
- ✂ Physical limits are facts, not questions: ~25 Hz Hue relay, ≤3 full
  flashes/s, hue flips travel round the wheel (never through grey).

## 2. Music response

- ❗ **Keep today's beat reactivity?** Any realism/motion change must keep
  "the good reactive movement". 🅰 Yes; show a beat lamp at several
  starting speeds. (fish-swim-plan)
- **Energy / intensity scaling:** 🅰 the drop's size and length scale with
  fire intensity (only Fish did until asked); charges build but still
  pulse; hits set rise speed, section intensity sets fade. (fish-lull-plan,
  transitions-drop-plan, single-led-power-plan)
- **Lull: keep today's or change it?** ❗ 🅰 Keep his existing lulls on the
  particle effects unless he names one as cheesy; he named Eye/Sauron
  ("keep the old one, but flip it vertically") and Squiggles (thinning
  out vs discharge, after smoothing the abrupt entry). Never propose one
  new lull for all 2D effects. (transitions-drop-plan, transitions-plan-rev)
- **Lull: how dark, how long dark?** 🅰 To black on the shared dark point,
  3 s cap, crystal and strips together; never half the lull dark with
  nothing on the panel (one searching keeper instead). (fish-lull-plan,
  strip-phase-plan)
- **Drop: how big, made of what?** 🅰 Out-shines the charge, starts on its
  mark (fires ~100 ms early), made of set colours, more pieces/brightness
  of colour, shockwave from the effect's own eye; no shockwave on an effect
  whose own drop is already big (Fireworks). (transitions-drop-plan,
  transitions-plan-rev3, strip-plan-rev3)
- **Breath-hold (short true black) before the drop?** Recommended 0.4 s as
  a setting; with a pre-drop it shifts to land right before the pre-drop.
  He overrode "drop it when a pre-drop plays". (transitions-drop-plan,
  predrop-plan)
- **Quiet / low level: what counts?** 🅰 The music's own captured level
  (quiet passages, fades, gaps), not his house volume. (fish-swim-plan)
- **Which scenes get the new music behaviour?** 🅰 Music scene only; the
  House scene (House Fish / House Star) keeps its calm look; build it as a
  per-scene setting so it can be turned on later. (fish-swim-plan,
  strip-phase-plan)
- **Pre-drop looks:** 🅰 low total light output is a hard value; strips
  stay dark (twinkle to "dim", peaks at full brightness, not a background
  fade); Black Hole uses the ring for every pre-drop kind; Star grows
  from the centre as it spins; Orbits jitter and grow on return. Ask only
  for an effect not yet covered. (predrop-plan)
- **Colour on the drop:** 🅰 every drop changes colour cleanly and
  immediately, ~150–210°; fade across the pre-drop when there is one,
  instant otherwise. (predrop-plan)

## 3. Colour sets and the rainbow limit

- 👁❗ **How does it wear each of his sets?** Show six of his sets side by
  side, calm and loud; for a looping/rainbow set show "as authored" vs
  each sorting (he asked for this unprompted twice). (sauron-eye-plan,
  sauron-eye-plan-v2)
- ❗ **White.** 🅰 No added white in charge, drop, wake or rim beyond what
  the set's own colour carries; layers compose by max/brighter-wins so a
  solid set cannot flood to white; opposite-hue pairs that add to white
  are a defect (the fish wake). (transitions-plan-rev3, strip-plan-rev3,
  fish-swim-plan)
- **Secondary colour (wake, trail, debris):** 🅰 the form's own colour,
  softer, "lighten" blend; a neighbouring hue is the fallback; never the
  opposite hue. (fish-swim-plan)
- **Palette sort default:** auto (brightness order for ordinary sets,
  cold→warm for rainbow), with authored/angle on the switch — his to
  confirm per effect at the tuning gate. (sauron-eye-plan-v2)
- **Rainbow select / colour jump limit:** a colour jump obeys the scene's
  rainbow select limit (PR #507); ask only whether a NEW kind should be
  exempt. 🅰 Not exempt.

## 4. Fixtures and devices

- **Which virtuals does the change reach?** Crystal / TV backlight +
  sconces strip / Singles / house single-colour virtuals. Confirm the set
  the scout found; never ask him to list them. (strip-phase-plan)
- **Count rule when forms differ** (3 fish → N orbits): 🅰 match on the way
  in, then the authored scene count wins a moment later; the authored
  count is his data. (shoal-burst-plan)
- ✂ Frame budget is a fact to report (ms/frame on 976 cells at 60 fps),
  not a question.

## 5. Transitions in and out

- 👁❗ **Which pairs, which direction first?** 🅰 Out-of-X first, then
  into-X; approval is given per direction in his own word. (star-plan,
  star-plan-rev)
- 👁❗ **Does the reverse mirror the forward?** 🅰 A reverse transition is
  the forward one with time inverted (a real program class, not a reversed
  video). (star-plan-rev)
- **Cut or blend?** 🅰 At the drop: a clean hard cut on the drop beat;
  never a crossfade through the lull between dissimilar effects; the
  incoming effect comes in loud. Between main effects outside a drop: a
  thematic program (release → flight → form) with velocity continuous.
  (drop-scene-variety-plan, transitions-drop-plan)
- **Program length:** may it run 1.2–2.5 s landing its anchor on the
  mark, or stay inside 0.5 s? Recommended 1.4 s per program. Still open
  on record; carry it as a param. (transitions-drop-plan)
- **When may a scene change land around a drop?** 🅰 On the drop; early at
  charge start only if the current scene has no good drop transition into
  the next or is stale; a flare inside the charge is also a good moment.
  (drop-scene-variety-plan)
- **Scope tier:** the named pairs get the full mechanism; the long tail
  gets only flight continuation. Ask "say the word if the tail should be
  left alone". (shoal-burst-plan)

## 6. Flares

- **A flare landing in a dark lull:** ❗ 🅰 the flare punches through (he
  overrode "dark wins"); dark resumes afterward. (strip-phase-plan,
  strip-plan-rev)
- **Which kinds punch / which kinds exist for this effect?** Structural:
  kinds that write to the device punch; name the new kind in the mould of
  an existing one (`firework_burst`, `sauron_surge`). Ask only the
  intensity floor. 🅰 ≥0.4 for a hue flip on the Singles. (sauron-eye-plan,
  single-led-power-plan)
- **Momentary vs permanent?** Recommend momentary with a hold for a poke,
  permanent for a colour move; confirm in one line.

## 7. Performance and targets

- ✂ Report, don't ask: per-frame cost on the build box for 976 cells and
  for the strip; Hue relay rate; WLED power limiter. Include the number in
  the plan's acceptance checks.

## 8. Testing and proof

- **Offline acceptance:** 🅰 byte-identical frames with the new keys off
  (the escape hatch); a pinned pre-change module as the red control; the
  explainer's measurements re-asserted by a test or `check_*.py`. Confirm
  the two or three numbers, not the method. (fish-swim-plan, strip-phase-plan,
  fish-lull-plan, bh-infall-plan)
- **Detection confidence first** (drop/pre-drop/word work): prove the rule
  on his confirmed events before building looks for it. 🅰 Yes. (predrop-plan)
- **Test Bed:** when the change is music-timing shaped, extend the Test
  Bed's lanes/knobs and score against his own triggers as ground truth;
  ask only which songs are the reference set. (transition-alignment-plan)

## 9. What "done" looks like

- ❗ **How will he judge it in the room?** Which scene, which song, what
  he watches for, how long, how to undo. Recommend a specific song he has
  used before (Dopamine's builds, Winny's spike gap, Caro, FINA for drop
  variety) and the escape-hatch param. He answers this one readily.
- **Taste numbers are params, not questions.** Ring hold, bloom speed,
  debris count, dart rate, thresholds: ship behind Sonic-reachable params
  with defaults from his tuning pass; list them in the plan as risks.
  🅰 Always. (star-plan, strip-plan-rev3, drop-scene-variety-plan)
- **What is NOT built:** name the deferred half (charge/lull/drop on a new
  effect "later after testing"; the 1-D twin; the House scene). 🅰 He
  defers choreography until he has lived with the first version.
  (sauron-eye-plan)
